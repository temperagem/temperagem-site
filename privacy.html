1. Who we are

"Temperagem Automacao" is a personal tool operated by the owner of the Temperagem cooking channel (the "Owner"). It is not a public service. It is used only by the Owner, on the Owner's own computer, with the Owner's own accounts.

2. Information we access

  • TikTok account information authorized by the Owner through TikTok Login Kit (basic profile information such as display name and account identifier).
  • Access tokens issued by TikTok, YouTube and Meta for the Owner's own accounts.
  • Video files and captions created by the Owner (the channel's own original content).

We do not collect or process personal data of any other person.

3. How information is used

Information is used only to connect the Owner's accounts and to send the Owner's own videos from YouTube to the Owner's social media accounts, including delivering a draft to the Owner's TikTok inbox.

4. Storage and security

Access tokens and settings are stored only on the Owner's computer and, when applicable, in private encrypted secret storage of the Owner's own code repository. They are not published, sold, shared or disclosed to third parties.

5. Sharing

We do not sell or share personal information. Data is transmitted only to the platforms the Owner chooses to use (TikTok, YouTube, Facebook and Instagram), strictly to perform the actions requested by the Owner. Each platform handles data under its own privacy policy.

6. Retention and deletion

Data is kept only as long as the Owner uses the tool. The Owner can revoke the app's access at any time in the TikTok app (Settings and privacy > Security & permissions > Apps and services) and delete stored tokens from the computer.

7. Children

This tool is not directed to children and is not used by anyone other than the Owner.

8. Changes

This policy may be updated from time to time. The latest version will always be available at this address.
