About

Temperagem is a cooking channel about Brazilian and international cuisine. Videos are published on YouTube, Facebook, Instagram and TikTok.

About the app "Temperagem Automacao"

Temperagem Automacao is a personal tool, used only by the owner of the Temperagem channel, to send the channel's own original videos from the owner's computer to the owner's own social media accounts, including TikTok. The app is not offered to the public and does not collect data from other people.

How the TikTok integration works

  1. The owner logs in once with their own TikTok account (Login Kit).
  2. When a new video is published on YouTube, the tool uploads the same video to the owner's TikTok inbox as a draft (Content Posting API, upload mode).
  3. The owner opens TikTok, reviews the caption, privacy and interaction settings, and publishes manually.
